# ParseFiend

Shows Parse Fiend Parse Points (PP) on player tooltips in World of Warcraft.

When you mouse over a player (in the world, in your Friends list, Communities/Guild,
or the Group Finder) ParseFiend looks up their current-tier DPS parse scores from a 
built-in database and adds a colored **Parse Points** line to the tooltip.

- **Alt / Shift / Ctrl** while hovering breaks the total down per difficulty
  (Mythic → Heroic → Normal → LFR).

### Commands
- `/pf` or `/parsefiend` — open settings
